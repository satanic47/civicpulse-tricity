Slide 02 • Problem Statement The Civic Infrastructure Gap

Why Municipal Grievance Systems Are Broken

Linguistic Disconnect

70%+ of grassroots citizens in Chandigarh, Mohali, and rural fringe wards express complaints verbally in Punjabi or Hindi, but legacy portals only accept rigid English form dropdowns.

Fragmented Data Silos

Grievances are scattered across WhatsApp, phone helplines, municipal portals, and social media. No consolidated source of truth exists across Tricity municipal boundaries.

Budget Misalignment

Policymakers allocate multi-crore ward budgets based on historical inertia rather than real-time citizen demand hotspots and infrastructure hazard urgency.

CivicPulse Tricity bridges the gap between grassroots citizen voice and municipal capital works.
Slide 03 • The Solution NagrikSetu AI Architecture

A Digital Public Good for Grassroots Governance

1. Zero-Friction Omnichannel Intake

Citizens simply send a voice note on WhatsApp or speak into the portal in Punjabi, Hindi, or English. Google Gemini transcribes, standardizes, geocodes, and triages the issue in sub-seconds.

2. Sector & Landmark Geo-Intelligence

Automated entity recognition maps colloquial landmarks ("Kiran Cinema", "Patel Market", "PU Gate 2") directly to Chandigarh Sector centroids, municipal wards, and administrative zones.

3. Demand Hotspot Clustering

Clustering algorithms aggregate individual complaints into high-density geographic demand clusters, computing localized urgency indices (1-10) and infrastructure health scores.

4. Municipal DPR Generator

Gemini AI analyzes hotspot density against ward census population and municipal budgets to generate executive Detailed Project Reports (DPR) for smart city engineering works.

Engineered under India's Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) principles: modular, open, and scalable.
Slide 04 • Technical Core Google AI Meaningful Role

How Google AI Drives End-to-End Execution

Gemini 1.5 Multilingual Reasoning & Normalization

Ingests diverse dialect transcripts (Punjabi Gurmukhi, Devanagari Hindi, Hinglish), detects sentiment, and translates into canonical structured English schemas with zero semantic loss.

Urgency Scoring & Departmental Routing

Evaluates life-safety hazard parameters (e.g. exposed live electric wires = 10/10, burst water mains = 9/10), routing directly to the responsible wing: Public Health, B&R Roads, Electricity, or Sanitation.

Open-Weight Gemma Local Fallback for Sovereign Security

Designed to run locally on municipal servers via open-weight Gemma 2, ensuring government data sovereignty with 100% offline uptime and zero per-token cloud costs.

Proven 100% test pass rate across 21 automated pytest integration benchmarks.
