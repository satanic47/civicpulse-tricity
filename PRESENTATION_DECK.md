## Slide 2: The Core Problem
1. **Linguistic Disconnect:** Over 70% of grassroots citizens in Chandigarh, Mohali, and fringe wards express grievances verbally in Punjabi or Hindi, while legacy portals require rigid English form dropdowns.
2. **Fragmented Silos:** Complaints live in disconnected channels—WhatsApp groups, phone calls, social media, and paper petitions.
3. **Budget & Spending Misalignment:** Municipal ward budgets are allocated without real-time citizen demand hotspot intelligence, leaving critical hazards unaddressed.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution — NagrikSetu AI
1. **Omnichannel Voice & Text Ingestion:** Accepts Punjabi, Hindi, and English via Voice Notes, WhatsApp, and Web.
2. **Chandigarh Sector Geo-Intelligence:** Resolves colloquial landmarks ("Kiran Cinema", "Patel Market", "PU Gate 2") directly to Le Corbusier sector grids (Sectors 1–60, Mohali Phases, Panchkula).
3. **Hotspot Density Clustering:** Identifies real-time infrastructure demand clusters and computes sector health scores (0–100).
4. **Municipal DPR Copilot:** Gemini AI aggregates citizen complaints with census population data to generate Detailed Project Reports (DPRs) with budget estimations for smart city engineers.

---

## Slide 4: Google AI's Meaningful Role
- **Gemini 1.5 Multilingual Reasoning:** Understands Punjabi Gurmukhi, Devanagari Hindi, and Hinglish, normalizing them into canonical structured schemas.
- **Urgency Scoring (1–10):** Detects life-safety hazards (e.g., exposed electric wires near schools = 10/10, burst water mains = 9/10).
- **Automated Departmental Routing:** Routes issues directly to the responsible wing: *B&R Roads, Public Health & Water, Electricity, or Sanitation*.
- **Open-Weight Gemma Fallback:** Open-weight architecture guarantees sovereign municipal deployment with 100% offline uptime and zero per-token cloud costs.
